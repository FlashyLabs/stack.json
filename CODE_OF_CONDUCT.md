# Code of Conduct

This is a small repository with a narrow purpose, and its conduct rules are
correspondingly short.

## The standard

- Be direct and be kind. Disagree with the work, not the person.
- Measure before you assert. Most disputes here are about whether a value is
  true; the answer is a reading, not an opinion, and the person who measured
  says what they measured and when.
- Report what happened, including when it is worse than expected. A wrong
  entry corrected loudly is worth more than a right one nobody checked.
- No harassment, no discrimination, no personal attacks, no publishing of
  anyone's private information. None of that is up for discussion.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where this repository
is being worked on, and any public space where someone is representing it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or refuse contributions and comments that break
this standard, and may block repeat offenders. Report a concern privately to
the address in `SECURITY.md`; it reaches the same people.

## Attribution

without adopting its text.
